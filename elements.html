Randall,C.F., Ullrich,P.A, and Goodnight, J.S. "Observational Uncertainties Across Contiguous USA Heatwave Related Metrics"

We contrast heatwave statistics in the contiguous USA across ten climate datasets, highlight regions of uncertainty, map regions of similar heatwave characteristics, and identify relationships between heatwave related metrics.

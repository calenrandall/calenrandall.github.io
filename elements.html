Publications

In-Review Publications

Randall,C.F., Ullrich,P.A, and Goodnight, J.S. "Observational Uncertainties Across Contiguous USA Heatwave Related Metrics"

We contrast heatwave statistics in the contiguous USA across ten climate datasets, highlight regions of uncertainty, map regions of similar heatwave characteristics, and identify relationships between heatwave related metrics.

Completed Publications

Non first author papers that I was involved in during graduate school at the University of California Davis.

Weather sensitivity to sea surface temperatures over the contiguous United States in a medium ensemble of high-resolution regionally refined simulations using E3SM

We evaluate the RRM-E3SM model against a gridded dataset (PRISM) for mean and extreme temperatures and precipitation and other climate extremes.

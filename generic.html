About Me

I am a fifth year Ph.D. student in the Land, Air, and Water Resources Department of UC Davis and a member of the DOE’s HyperFACETS team. My research is centered upon statistically evaluating heatwaves in the contiguous USA. More specifically, I compare heatwave metrics across multiple climate data products (models, reanalysis, gridded, etc), identify regions of similar heatwave characteristics, quantify relationships between heatwave characteristics and drivers, and analyze the impact of oceanic modes of variability on heatwave metrics. In addition to heatwaves, I evaluate the impact of oceanic modes of variability on annual and seasonal precipitation and extreme precipitation events.
